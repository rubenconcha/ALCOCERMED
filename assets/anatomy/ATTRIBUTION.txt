GLUTEAL EDUCATIONAL EXTENSION (2026-10-12)
Inferior gluteal nerve is absent from the original NervousSystem100 mesh and Unity prefab. AlcoCerMed added bilateral schematic 3D curves with terminal branches following the original deep gluteus maximus surface. These are educational reconstructions, not segmented source anatomy, and do not represent rootlets, exact branching or individual variations. Original arteries were retained without alteration.
Reference: Saladin, chapter 10, p. 361 (PDF 389).
